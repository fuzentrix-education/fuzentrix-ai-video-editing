# Kinetic Typography

**Availability:** Content-driven. **Used for:** the term being defined, a rule, a key takeaway, 'X vs Y'.

All colours, fonts and timings come from the brand kit (CSS variables `--fz-*`), so this family looks the same in every video and adapts to the brand.

## Look

- Outfit 900, 64–92 px; the key word in the brand gradient (`--fz-grad`).
- **12 words at most, never running speech** (that would be subtitles).
- At most one kinetic moment per section.

## Motion

- Words rise 60% of their height with power4.out, 0.12 s apart or on each spoken word.
- Lines wipe in left-to-right (clip-path).

Templates: see `global/templates/kinetic/`.
